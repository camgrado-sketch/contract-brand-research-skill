# D1–D6 Research Dimensions

Choose only the dimensions needed by the confirmed Alignment Brief. A focused commercial question usually needs three or four dimensions, not all six.

| Dimension | Core question | Hard indicators | Interpretive prompts |
|---|---|---|---|
| D1 — Positioning and narrative | What does the brand claim to improve, for whom, and in what settings? | Founding year, stated purpose, official taglines | Is the story human, technical, cultural, operational, or status-led? |
| D2 — Product system and platform | What repeatable product platform generates the offer? | Series, variants, SKUs, configuration options, digital product data | Is it a system, a family, a collection of icons, or project customization? |
| D3 — CMF and craft | What physical choices make the value credible? | Material groups, finish options, certifications, technical features | Which details support durability, comfort, repair, tactility, or perceived quality? |
| D4 — Commercial and channel model | How does the brand reach specification, transaction, delivery, and service? | Subsidiaries, dealers, showrooms, export share, employees, service sites | Which role is played by A&D, dealer, manufacturer, installer, and owner? |
| D5 — Projects and customers | Who buys, for what recurring space requirement, and through what decision chain? | Cases, sectors, named clients, project types, quantity cues | Is demand driven by owner, operator, architect, procurement, or public tender? |
| D6 — Premium and defensibility | Why is a higher price accepted and what makes substitution costly? | Warranty, product life, spare parts, certifications, renewal service | Is the premium based on risk reduction, specification, experience, identity, or operations? |

## Dimension combinations by question

| Research question | Required dimensions | Useful optional dimensions |
|---|---|---|
| Explain a business model | D1, D4, D6 | D5 |
| Learn from product development | D2, D3 | D1, D6 |
| Design a channel strategy | D4, D5 | D6 |
| Build a complete brand view | D1–D6 | None |
| Explain price versus scale | D2, D4, D5, D6 | D1 |
| Explain public-space relevance | D1, D2, D5 | D3, D6 |

## Self-brand mapping prompts

Use only after the external analysis is complete, and keep the output separate from market facts.

| Prompt | Output |
|---|---|
| Which capability is absent in the self-brand today? | Gap list |
| Which gap is feasible to build within one product cycle? | Near-term actions |
| Which capability requires ecosystem, channel, or installed-base scale? | Structural constraints |
| Where does the self-brand have a genuine alternative advantage? | Differentiation hypothesis |
| If only one capability can be prioritized, what decision changes first? | Priority recommendation |
